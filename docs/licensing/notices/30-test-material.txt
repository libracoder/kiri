Test material from other projects
=================================

None of this is part of any Kiri binary. It is test input. The golden
vectors and recorded protocol traffic the builder's tests also read are not
in this repository; they are kept, with their own notices, in the builder
test data that scripts/fetch-test-data.sh fetches.

Test inputs of the image builder
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The image builder's own tests, and the test inputs they take from BuildKit,
containerd/platforms, moby/patternmatcher and Go, are in the kiri-forge
repository (https://github.com/libracoder/kiri-forge) and credited in its
NOTICE.
