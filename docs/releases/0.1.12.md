## New
- **Linux: Kiri sets itself up when it opens.** Before the main window, Kiri checks that its engine is installed and running, that your account can use it, that `docker` talks to Kiri and that builds work, with a button to fix each. Your password is asked once, by the system's own prompt, and your account can use Kiri straight away: no logging out and back in. Kiri only asks you to log out on a system that can't apply the access right away, and then gives you Log out and Restart buttons. From a terminal, `sudo kiri host-access $USER` does the same.

## Fixed
- **Stop stays stopped.** Stop used to put Kiri to sleep, and the next build, `docker` command or share woke it again within seconds. Now Stop stops every container, closes your shares and keeps Kiri off until you press Start, even across restarts of your computer; a `docker` command in the meantime says Kiri is stopped. Kiri still sleeps by itself when it's idle and wakes when you need it, as before.
- **Linux: stopping, restarting or removing Kiri leaves nothing running.** Until now systemd's stop ended Kiri's engine at once, without its shutdown, so containers were left running after `systemctl stop kiri` or uninstalling the package, and `systemctl restart kiri` could report a failure. The engine now stops its containers and cleans up first.
- **Linux under WSL: containers reach the internet every time.** Containers asked WSL's DNS forwarder directly, and about a third of their lookups went unanswered ("bad address", timeouts). They now resolve through Kiri, which asks it from the host's side.
- **Long image tags** no longer get cut off in the Images list: a tag that is a commit hash shows its first 12 characters, other long tags end in "…", and the whole tag is on hover.

## Better
- **Click a tag to copy it**, the whole tag, without opening the image.
- **The Containers list leaves out docker build's BuildKit workers** (`buildx_buildkit_…`): they're the build machinery, not containers you ran. Your builds are still under Builds.

## Known issue
- **`--add-host name:host-gateway` with BuildKit** still fails, with buildx's own error, because buildx only allows it for Docker's built-in builder. Use the gateway address itself, or `DOCKER_BUILDKIT=0`. Kiri's own built-in builder will fix it in a later release.
